1.1 Beyond "The Skeleton"

In every beginner tutorial, HTML is called the "Skeleton" of a website. This is a massive oversimplification. HTML isn't just a frame; it is the Information Architechture.

  • What you think:HTML is for making text bold or adding images.
  • The Reality:HTML is a communication protocol for machines. It tells web crawler what is important, tells screen readers how to navigate for the blind, and tells browsers how to prioritize loading
